1. Service Description

VoxSync ("Service") is a real-time voice translation platform provided by Luvent Technologies ("we", "us", "our") that enables bidirectional translation during telephone calls over PSTN and VoIP networks.

The Service operates as an intermediary translation layer between telephony endpoints using speech-to-text, machine translation, and text-to-speech technologies.

3. Telephony Service Provisions

3.1 Call Routing and Processing

VoxSync operates as a telecommunications service intermediary. When you initiate a call using the Service:

  • Your call is routed through our infrastructure via Twilio Media Streams
  • Audio is processed in real-time using G.711 μ-law encoding at 8000 Hz
  • Voice data is streamed through speech recognition, translation, and synthesis pipelines
  • Translated audio is delivered to the destination endpoint in real-time

3.2 Audio Quality and Latency

The Service targets sub-400ms end-to-end latency. However, actual latency depends on network conditions, geographic distance, and carrier infrastructure. We make no guarantees regarding call quality, latency, or translation accuracy.

3.3 Carrier Compliance

Your use of the Service must comply with all applicable telecommunications regulations in your jurisdiction, including but not limited to call recording consent laws, GDPR, and carrier-specific terms of service.

4. Audio Data Handling

4.1 Ephemeral Processing

VoxSync processes audio data in memory only. We do not persistently store, record, or retain voice data by default:

  • Audio streams are processed in real-time and discarded immediately after transmission
  • Transcripts generated during translation are ephemeral and not stored beyond session duration
  • Session metadata (call duration, language pairs, timestamps) may be retained for billing and operational purposes
